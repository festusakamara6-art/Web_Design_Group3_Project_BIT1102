About us

SaloneTech Hub is a student-led web studio based in Freetown. Our problem: many local businesses have no online presence. Our solution: simple, professional websites that are easy to maintain.

🎯 Mission

Make quality web design accessible to everyone in Sierra Leone.

👁️ Vision

A digitally connected community of creators and businesses.

🛠️ Technologies

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Figma, GitHub and online deployment.
